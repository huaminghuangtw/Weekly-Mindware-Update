# Things I Learned This Week

* [Keeping up with the Joneses](https://www.google.com/search?q=Keeping+up+with+the+Joneses) means trying to match the wealth, social status, or material possessions of your neighbors or friends.
* [瓊漿玉液](https://www.google.com/search?q=瓊漿玉液)：原指古代神話中，用美玉製成、喝了可以成仙的漿液。現用來比喻香醇的美酒或甘美的汁液。
* **New Money** is a social class of the rich whose wealth has been acquired within their own generation, rather than by familial inheritance. ↔ **Old Money (上流社會)**
* Belt-and-braces (雙保險) is a British idiom that means using two or more extra safety actions to be very careful, even when only one action is enough.
* A [silver lining](https://www.google.com/search?q=silver+lining)【(逆境或不幸中的) 一點慰藉、一線希望】is a hopeful, positive aspect or advantage that comes out of a difficult, unpleasant, or unfortunate situation.
