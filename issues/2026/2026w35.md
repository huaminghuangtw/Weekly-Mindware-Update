# Things I Learned This Week

* 尼特族 [NEET](https://www.google.com/search?q=NEET)（Not in Education, Employment, or Training）泛指 15–34 歲未就學、未就業、未培訓的年輕人。與中國「躺平族」、日本「繭居族」概念交疊。
* The [N-back task](https://www.google.com/search?q=N-back+task) is a popular cognitive test and brain-training exercise used to measure and improve working memory. During the test, you are shown a continuous sequence of items (e.g., letters, numbers, or shapes). Your goal is to identify if the current item matches the one that appeared N steps back in the sequence.
* [人渣盒](https://www.google.com/search?q=人渣盒) = 爬蟲飼養盒/飼育箱
* 「微縮片」（[Microfilm](https://www.google.com/search?q=Microfilm)）是一種將紙本文件以高度縮小的攝影方式保存於底片上的技術。在數位化普及之前，它是圖書館、政府機關、報社最重要的長期保存媒介之一。
* [Retirement House](https://www.google.com/search?q=Retirement+House) = 老人院 = 養老院
